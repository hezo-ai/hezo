# Getting started on Hezo Cloud

Signing up takes two things: a sentence about what you want your team to work
on, and an email address. There is no password and no credit card.

## 1. Describe the work, and give us your email

Start from **Launch team** on [hezo.ai](https://hezo.ai), or go straight to
[app.hezo.ai/start](https://app.hezo.ai/start). The form asks what your team
should work on. Write a few lines: what the project is, who it is for, and what
done looks like. That brief becomes your first project, so it is worth more
than a title.

The language you fill the form in becomes your account's language, and your
instance's.

## 2. Open the link we email you

We send a link rather than asking you to choose a password. It works once,
expires after fifteen minutes, and works from any browser - so you can start on
a phone and finish on a laptop.

There is no password anywhere on Hezo Cloud. Signing in later means asking for
another link.

## 3. Choose your address

Your instance answers on `you.app.hezo.ai`, and you pick the `you` part. Three
to thirty characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

**The address cannot be changed later, and a name is never reused** - including
after an account is deleted. Choose one you will still want in a year.

Your instance is built the moment you take the address, which takes a few
minutes. We email you when it is ready.

## 4. Choose your recovery phrase

The first time you open your instance it asks you to create a **twelve-word
recovery phrase**. Everything sensitive your instance holds is encrypted behind
it.

That phrase is generated in your browser and never reaches us. **We do not hold
a copy, and nobody here can recover it for you.** Write it down somewhere
durable before you continue - a password manager, or paper in a drawer. See
[Master key & encryption](/docs/security/master-key) for what it protects.

You will be asked for it again if your instance restarts.

## 5. Meet your team

Your brief opens as your first project, and the CEO picks it up from there -
scoping the work with you and staffing a team for it. From here the rest of
these docs apply: see [Your first project](/docs/getting-started/first-project)
for what to expect, and [What's different from
self-hosting](/docs/cloud/whats-different) for the handful of pages that do not.

Your free trial starts when you take your address, not when you first sign in.
See [Plans, usage & billing](/docs/cloud/plans-and-billing).
