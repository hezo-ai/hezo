**What "current" means.** Three things must hold before any figure in a deep-dive is trusted, whether you are producing it or checking it:

- **Price.** The stated market price is within two trading days, the 52-week range is consistent with it, and market cap reconciles to stated shares times stated price. Never carry a price forward from a prior revision. Every price-anchored figure - market cap, enterprise value, multiples, scenario returns - is recomputed from it.
- **Disclosures.** No filing, shareholder letter or report more recent than the ones cited exists. A figure can be correct against its stated source while the source itself is superseded, and time-sensitive metrics shift sharply between reporting periods.
- **Events.** Material statements since the most recent filing are reflected. Executives disclose timelines and milestones at industry events and in broadcast interviews, especially on earnings day, before anything reaches a filing. Credit only reporting attributed to a named company representative, flag anonymous sourcing as such, and choose outlets by the company's own sector and market rather than a fixed list.

A document current against filings but missing material event-based disclosures is still stale.
